SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
Nutrition Consultation & Patient Management Web Application
Version 1.0  |  14 October 2026  |  Draft for stakeholder approval
Working product name: NutriCare Pro. This specification consolidates the previously planned 18 development modules; actual implementation must be verified against the repository.
1. Purpose, scope and objectives
Deliver a responsive, secure web application for a qualified nutrition professional to attract prospective patients, schedule consultations, deliver personalized nutrition plans, and maintain patient engagement. The system provides public marketing pages, a patient portal, a nutritionist workspace and an administrator console.
Primary outcomes: accessible booking, reduced administrative workload, safe handling of health records, and measurable follow-up engagement.
In scope: authentication, appointments, intake, medical documents, diet plans, progress, payments, messaging, video-session links, content, notifications, analytics and audit trails.
Out of scope unless separately approved: medical diagnosis, emergency triage, automated clinical decision-making, insurance claims and multi-clinic marketplace support.
2. Stakeholders and roles
[TABLE]
Role | Responsibilities / access
Visitor | Browse services, biography, articles, pricing, FAQs and start booking.
Patient | Manage own profile, appointments, payments, records, plans and communication.
Nutritionist | Manage assigned patients, schedule, consultations, diet plans and progress.
Administrator | Manage users, content, configurations, reporting and support; clinical data access limited by policy.
[/TABLE]
3. Functional requirements
FR-01 Public website & discovery
Responsive home, about, credentials, services, fees, FAQs, contact and blog pages.
Prominent Book Appointment CTAs, service-specific landing pages, SEO metadata and sitemap.
Publish only verified qualifications, authentic testimonials and approved clinical claims.
FR-02 Identity & profiles
Patient registration, email verification, sign-in/out, password reset and profile update.
Role-based authorization for Patient, Nutritionist and Admin; server-side ownership checks.
Session expiry, token revocation, login rate limits and optional MFA for privileged accounts.
FR-03 Booking & availability
Nutritionist configures working hours, breaks, holidays, service durations and consultation modes.
Patients select service, available slot, time zone, complete intake and receive confirmation.
Prevent overlapping bookings atomically; reschedule/cancel using configurable cutoffs.
Support appointment lifecycle: Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, Completed, No-show.
FR-04 Patient intake and clinical records
Collect relevant goals, history, allergies, medications and lifestyle with explicit consent.
Patient can upload permitted reports; clinician can review and add dated consultation notes.
Access controls, retention and deletion rules apply to sensitive health information.
FR-05 Diet plans
Nutritionist creates, versions, edits, assigns and archives individualized plans.
Plans contain meal schedules, portions, nutrition estimates, instructions and review dates.
Patient views current and previous plans and downloads accessible PDFs.
FR-06 Progress monitoring
Record weight, measurements, adherence and clinician-selected metrics over time.
Display dated charts and trends; label estimated calculations and avoid diagnostic claims.
Nutritionist reviews progress and adjusts plans with change history.
FR-07 Payments & invoicing
Show consultation/package prices and checkout before payment.
Record payment intent, pending/success/failure/refund and generate receipts.
Verify provider-signed callbacks; use idempotency keys and never store card credentials.
FR-08 Messaging & consultation
Authorized patient–nutritionist conversations with persistent message history.
Unread counts, real-time delivery, reconnection and attachment restrictions.
Create protected video-session links and enforce appointment access policies.
FR-09 Notifications
Email/SMS/in-app confirmations, reminders, reschedules and new-plan alerts.
Notification preferences, delivery status, retry policy and message templates.
Avoid exposing sensitive medical details in external notifications.
FR-10 Content and administration
Admin manages services, pricing, availability settings, FAQs, blogs and moderation.
Dashboards for bookings, payments and operational metrics with least-privilege data access.
Audit important account, appointment, plan, document and payment actions.
FR-11 Support and feedback
Contact form with spam prevention and acknowledgement.
Patient feedback and testimonials require consent and moderation.
Publish clear emergency disclaimer: service is not for urgent or emergency care.
4. Key workflows and acceptance criteria
[TABLE]
Workflow | Acceptance criteria
New patient booking | User selects an actually free slot; booking appears in both dashboards; confirmation issued once; concurrent attempts cannot double-book.
Diet plan assignment | Only authorized clinician can assign; patient sees the latest published version; revisions remain traceable.
Report upload | Invalid type/oversized file rejected; valid file stored privately; only authorized patient and clinician can access.
Payment confirmation | A booking is marked paid only after verified provider confirmation; duplicate webhooks do not duplicate transactions.
Cross-account access | Patient A cannot read Patient B's appointments, messages, plans or documents by altering IDs.
Cancellation | Cancellation policy is displayed and applied; slot release, refunds and notifications follow configured rules.
[/TABLE]
5. Non-functional requirements
[TABLE]
ID | Requirement / target
NFR-01 Security | TLS everywhere; encryption at rest for sensitive data; secrets in managed secret store; OWASP-oriented controls.
NFR-02 Privacy | Explicit privacy notice, consent records, data minimization, export/deletion workflows and documented retention policy.
NFR-03 Availability | Proposed production target 99.5% monthly uptime; monitored health checks and incident response.
NFR-04 Performance | Proposed p95 API response under 500 ms for routine reads at agreed load; public Core Web Vitals monitored.
NFR-05 Accessibility | Target WCAG 2.2 AA for core journeys, keyboard support, contrast and screen-reader labels.
NFR-06 Compatibility | Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; responsive from 320 px upward.
NFR-07 Reliability | Automated backups, tested restore, retry-safe notifications and payment webhook idempotency.
NFR-08 Maintainability | Modular architecture, migrations, documented APIs, CI checks and structured logs without clinical secrets.
NFR-09 Localization | Pakistan Standard Time default; store instants in UTC; prepare for English/Urdu content.
NFR-10 Observability | Traceable request IDs, audit logs, error monitoring and alerting; redact personal/medical data.

7. Core data model
Identity: User, Role, PatientProfile, NutritionistProfile, ConsentRecord, RefreshToken.
Scheduling: Service, AvailabilityRule, AvailabilityException, Appointment, ConsultationNote.
Care: IntakeForm, MedicalReport, DietPlan, DietPlanVersion, MealItem, ProgressEntry.
Commerce: Price, Payment, Refund, Invoice, ProviderWebhookEvent.
Engagement: Conversation, Message, Notification, BlogPost, Testimonial.
Governance: AuditLog, AccessLog, RetentionPolicy, SystemSetting.
Every patient-linked record must enforce ownership/assignment; use immutable IDs, timestamps, concurrency protection and appropriate indexes. Document entity relationships and migration history separately.
8. UI/UX requirements
Visual direction: premium, calm, science-led wellness; white backgrounds, emerald/teal accents, accessible contrast and consistent spacing.
Landing page: credible hero, verified qualifications, concise services, consultation formats, authentic reviews, FAQs and visible booking CTA.
Booking wizard: service → date/time → intake → payment if applicable → confirmation; show progress and recovery from errors.
Patient dashboard: next appointment, current diet plan, progress chart, secure reports and messages.
Nutritionist dashboard: today’s schedule, patient queue, plan editor, clinical notes and follow-ups.
Admin dashboard: operational metrics, user/content management, audit access and configuration.
Mobile-first layout; clear form validation, loading/empty states, keyboard access and no manipulative medical marketing.
9. Security, ethics and compliance
Verify applicable Pakistan health-data, professional licensing, consumer-protection and electronic-payment requirements with qualified local counsel before launch.
Use explicit consent for sensitive information, teleconsultation, testimonials and marketing communications.
Never promise cures or guaranteed weight loss; clearly distinguish nutrition counseling from emergency medical services.
Do not allow AI-generated advice to be issued as a clinical plan without professional review.
Define who may access clinical notes, how long records are retained, and how patients exercise data rights.
Conduct authorization, upload, injection, XSS, CSRF (where relevant), webhook and audit-integrity testing.
10. Testing and release gates
Automated unit and integration tests for domain rules, validation, permissions and booking concurrency.
Playwright E2E journeys for visitor, patient, nutritionist and administrator on desktop/mobile.
API contract tests for authentication, appointments, plans, files, payments and notifications.
Staging smoke tests of email/SMS, payment sandbox, video links, backups and restore.
Accessibility and performance audits; penetration testing or independent security review before handling real medical records.
Release only after all critical/high defects are resolved, privacy policies approved and clinician signs off on workflows.
11. Deployment and operations
Separate local, staging and production environments with independent secrets and databases.
CI pipeline: restore, build, lint, unit tests, integration tests, migration checks, deploy to staging, smoke test, approved production promotion.
Daily backups and documented recovery objectives; configure uptime, error-rate and payment-failure alerts.
Operational runbooks for account lockout, missed reminders, failed payment callbacks, data incidents and disaster recovery.
